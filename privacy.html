Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains how HiddenForm handles information when you play on iPhone, iPad or Android, visit this website or contact support.

Your puzzle progress

The app stores puzzle progress, sorting positions and preferences on your device. No game account is required, and we do not operate a cloud progress service. Deleting the app removes these local records. A rewarded video can temporarily unlock a puzzle. Your saved progress stays on this device when the unlock expires.

Advertising and your choices

Lion, Elephant and Zebra are always free. In versions with a free Today’s Pick, you can open the daily puzzle without watching a video. A banner may appear at the puzzle table, and you can keep playing if it doesn’t load. Other standard puzzles may offer a rewarded video to unlock play. Once your Remove Ads purchase is verified, standard puzzles open without videos or gameplay banners. Premium collections are sold separately.

Unity LevelPlay connects the game to advertising partners. Unity Ads and ironSource can supply gameplay banners. Google AdMob can also supply rewarded ads through LevelPlay. These services may process device and advertising identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location it indicates, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance data. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the services working. See Unity’s player privacy policy and Google’s advertising information.

We use InMobi Choice to present applicable privacy choices and communicate them to advertising partners. The game checks your choices before requesting a gameplay banner. You can revisit your ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings. On iPhone and iPad, you can decline Apple’s tracking request and still play or earn available video rewards. See InMobi’s privacy policy.

Where your privacy choices permit, we use Meta’s SDK to measure our advertising campaigns. Meta may receive device identifiers and events such as app installs, puzzle activity, purchases, and the format and revenue of ads shown. See Meta’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify the Remove ads entitlement and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles billing information under Apple’s privacy policy.

On Android, Google Play processes purchases. The app uses Google Play Billing to verify and restore the Remove ads purchase. A signed purchase record is stored locally to remember verified access when offline. We do not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase-account server. Google handles billing information under its privacy policy.

Android artwork downloads

The first three puzzles are included with the app. Additional artwork is delivered by Google Play when you select it. Google may process technical information needed to deliver those downloads. Downloaded artwork can be removed in the app without deleting your saved puzzle progress. We do not operate a separate artwork-download tracking service.

Support messages

If you email carlos@kyndlo.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a description of an issue or a screenshot. We use this information to respond and resolve support requests. We retain it only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. The download page offers optional Google Analytics for visits, campaign sources and store-button clicks. We load Analytics only after you allow it; you can revisit Analytics choices on the download page. Automatic mobile routing works without opting in. Campaign labels travel to Apple and Google Play for their acquisition reports. See our website analytics notice for cookies, data processing and choices. We do not add website advertising scripts. GitHub may collect technical information, including your IP address, to deliver and secure its hosting service. Read the GitHub privacy statement.

Your choices and questions

You can delete local game data by deleting the app. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information you provided through support, or ask about other privacy rights that apply where you live. For information independently handled by Apple or Google, use their privacy controls or contact those providers.

The game is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact Kyndlo LLC at carlos@kyndlo.com. We may update this policy as the app or its services change; the effective date above identifies the latest revision.
