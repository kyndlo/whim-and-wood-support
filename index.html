A little help at the puzzle table

Let’s put it together.

Need help with Whim & Wood? We’re here for questions, purchase issues and anything that gets in the way of a good puzzle.

Email support ↗

carlos@kyndlo.com · Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I move around the table?

Drag an empty part of the table with one finger to move the view. Pinch to zoom. Drag a puzzle piece to move it; it lifts slightly above your finger so you can see where it goes. Nearby matching pieces connect magnetically.

Where are the reference picture and menus?

Puzzles open in fullscreen. Tap the picture icon at the top left for the reference image. Use the menu button at the top right to show table controls, including Fit table, Spread out, hints and settings. The outline guide is optional in settings.

Can I enjoy or restart a finished puzzle?

After the completion animation, pinch to zoom into the finished picture and drag to explore it. Choose Restart puzzle to start again, or choose another animal. A puzzle accessed through a video requires a new video to restart unless you have Remove ads.

Which puzzles are free?

The first three puzzles are included without ads. For other puzzles, completing an available rewarded video grants access for one table visit. Leaving the table or restarting requires another video; your saved puzzle progress remains.

How do Remove ads and Restore purchases work?

Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes video requirements for every puzzle. To recover an existing purchase, sign in with the Apple Account used to buy it and choose Restore purchases in the app’s Remove ads offer. Apple handles payments and refunds; request a refund through Apple.

A video isn’t loading or didn’t unlock a puzzle.

Videos need an internet connection and may not always be available. Access is granted only after the advertising service confirms that you earned the reward; closing a video early does not unlock the puzzle. Try again later or enjoy one of the three free puzzles. Contact us if a completed video did not grant access.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and preferences are saved on your device. Whim & Wood does not have an account or cloud progress service. Deleting the app removes local progress, so please contact us before reinstalling if you want help with a problem.
